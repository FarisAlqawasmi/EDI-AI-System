What is EDI?

Equality, Diversity, and Inclusion (EDI) refers to policies and practices that promote fair treatment and full participation of all people, particularly groups that have historically been underrepresented or subject to discrimination. This platform provides access to curated EDI resources and AI-powered question answering to help researchers, educators, and practitioners navigate EDI research and best practices.
