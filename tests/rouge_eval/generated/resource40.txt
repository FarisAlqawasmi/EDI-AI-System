The Best Practice Guide: Developing Inclusive Conferences, created by the University of Oxford's School of Geography and the Environment, is designed to help conference organisers and institutions foster diversity, accessibility, and inclusivity in all aspects of event planning and delivery. It offers practical steps for pre-event planning, speaker selection, session participation, networking, communication, and harassment prevention, with a focus on gender, ethnicity, disability, and caring responsibilities. Key recommendations include choosing accessible venues, diversifying speaker line-ups and formats, providing financial support such as bursaries and flexible pricing, and implementing codes of conduct with clear reporting mechanisms. The guide also stresses the importance of inclusive communication, mentoring underrepresented groups, and collaboration between organisers and institutions to create welcoming, equitable conference environments. Overall, it supports organisers in creating events where all attendees feel respected, able to contribute, and represented.
