This resource highlights an event hosted by Oxford Brookes on 5 June 2023, focused on promoting equity, diversity, and inclusion (EDI) by addressing the gender gap in STEM and university spinout companies. It is intended for women researchers, founders of university spinouts, women Business Angels and investors, and key stakeholders in the enterprise ecosystem. The event brought together influential organizations such as Innovate UK, the Royal Academy of Engineering, and the British Business Bank to discuss challenges and opportunities for women in STEM entrepreneurship. A panel discussion and Q&A are available to provide insights into how women scientists and entrepreneurs are tackling global problems. The resource serves as a platform to support women backing women in STEM investments and innovation.
