The Tech Talent Charter (TTC) Toolkit reflects on the journey and closure of the UK non-profit dedicated to advancing diversity and inclusion (D&I) in the tech sector. It is primarily aimed at tech employers, government bodies, educators, and organizations committed to improving D&I in technology. The resource highlights key challenges such as stalled progress in diverse representation, reduced D&I budgets, and the merging of D&I efforts into broader initiatives, which threaten sustainability. TTC’s work included sharing best practices, analyzing diversity data from a large tech workforce, and significantly increasing female representation in tech roles. The closure serves as a call to action for the industry to renew focus and investment in D&I to drive sustainable change in the future.
