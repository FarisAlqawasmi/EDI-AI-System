STEM Equals is a four-year research and impact project funded by EPSRC under the Inclusion Matters initiative, aimed at fostering more inclusive STEM communities for women and LGBT individuals in academia and industry. The resource is intended for stakeholders in higher education and STEM industries who are interested in addressing systemic inequalities. Using an intersectional approach, the project examines working cultures and the challenges faced by women and LGBT staff in STEM fields. In collaboration with BAM Nuttall Ltd, STEM Equals will develop and assess strategic initiatives to enhance equality and diversity within STEM environments.
