This briefing from the All-Party Parliamentary Group (APPG) on Diversity and Inclusion in STEM provides strategies to advance equality, diversity, and inclusion (EDI) within the STEM workforce. It is aimed at STEM leaders, organisations, policymakers, HR professionals, and stakeholders involved in workforce and business strategy development. Key points emphasize embedding EDI as central to business growth with measurable targets, improving diversity data collection and use, evaluating EDI interventions, and addressing challenges faced by start-ups, SMEs, and universities. The resource highlights the importance of strong leadership commitment, integrating EDI with business strategies, supporting staff networks, and early outreach to schools. It also calls for government and sector collaboration to enhance compliance, data quality, and sharing of best practices to foster a more inclusive STEM sector.
