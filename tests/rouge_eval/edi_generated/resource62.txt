The STEM Equals project is a four-year research and impact initiative funded by EPSRC under the Inclusion Matters programme. It is designed to create more inclusive STEM environments for women and LGBT+ people in both academia and industry. Aimed at researchers, institutions, and industry partners, the project uses an intersectional approach to examine workplace cultures, identify systemic inequalities, and develop evidence-based strategies for change. Key goals include improving hiring and promotion practices, supporting inclusive policies, and enhancing retention and belonging for underrepresented groups. The project is led by the University of Strathclyde in partnership with BAM Nuttall Ltd.
