The Women and Spinouts project, led by Oxford Brookes University in collaboration with the University of Oxford, investigates the barriers and enablers for women in STEM to lead university spinout companies. Funded by the EPSRC under the Inclusion Matters programme, the project is aimed at researchers, academic institutions, investors, and policymakers. It highlights the underrepresentation of women in spinout leadership and explores systemic challenges across the entrepreneurial pathway. Through research reports, interviews, and events, the project offers insights and recommendations to support gender-inclusive innovation. Its goal is to foster a more equitable spinout ecosystem by influencing policy, practice, and investment strategies.
