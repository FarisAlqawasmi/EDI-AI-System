This guidance, developed by the UK Cabinet Office’s Disability Unit, provides advice on using respectful and inclusive language when referring to disabled people. It is intended for anyone writing or speaking about disability, especially in public, professional, or policy contexts. The resource emphasizes avoiding outdated or negative terms, using person-first and empowering language, and being mindful of tone and everyday expressions. It also includes a list of preferred terms and practical tips for respectful communication and behaviour.
