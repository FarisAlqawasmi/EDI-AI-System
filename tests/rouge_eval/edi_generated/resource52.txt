The Tech Talent Charter Toolkit is a free, practical resource designed to help organisations improve diversity and inclusion (D&I) in the tech sector. It is aimed at employers of all sizes, HR professionals, and D&I leaders. The toolkit includes best practice guides, case studies, and actionable tools covering topics such as inclusive hiring, retention, progression, neurodiversity, and allyship. Developed collaboratively with industry partners, it supports organisations in creating more equitable and inclusive workplaces.
