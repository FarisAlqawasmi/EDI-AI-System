The Equality, Diversity, and Inclusion Strategies in STEM – Briefing (April 2025) explores how STEM organisations develop and implement EDI strategies, based on interviews with EDI and HR leaders. It is aimed at policymakers, STEM employers, and EDI professionals. The report highlights four key areas for action: embedding EDI into business growth, improving diversity data, evaluating EDI impact, and supporting smaller organisations. It stresses the importance of leadership, better data practices, and inclusive workplace cultures. The briefing also calls for tailored support for start-ups and more meaningful benchmarking and accountability across the sector.
